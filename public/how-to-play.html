How to Play

The goal

Choose a shape, then draw its outline in one continuous motion on the canvas. The closer your stroke is to the target shape, the higher your percentage score. A perfect score is difficult on purpose; tiny differences in spacing, closure, and smoothness matter.

How scoring works

The score combines several checks. Shape accuracy compares your path to the target outline. Coverage checks whether you traveled around the full shape instead of drawing only part of it. Closure checks whether the end of your stroke returns close to the beginning. Smoothness rewards a steady movement, while the winding check prevents extra loops from scoring well.

Shape tips

For circles, try to keep a consistent distance from the center. For squares and diamonds, focus on steady sides and clean turns. For triangles, avoid rounding off the corners too much. For hexagons, aim for six balanced sides rather than a rushed loop.

Leaderboard

Each shape has a global top five. If you qualify, you can enter a leaderboard name. The same name can appear once per shape; submitting a better score updates that entry. If your score exactly ties the final leaderboard spot, the game uses a coin flip to decide who keeps the spot.
