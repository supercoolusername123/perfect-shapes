Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Nail the Shape is a browser game that collects only the information needed to run the game, remember scores, and operate the leaderboard.

Information you provide

If you submit a global leaderboard score, we store the leaderboard name you enter, the shape, the score, and the time the score was created. Please do not enter private personal information as your leaderboard name.

Information stored on your device

The game may use local storage in your browser to remember personal bests and the last leaderboard name you entered. This information stays on your device unless you choose to submit a score to the global leaderboard.

Advertising and analytics

This site may use Google AdSense and related Google services. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, measure performance, prevent abuse, and comply with legal requirements. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google's partner sites policy.

Contact

If you have a privacy question or want a leaderboard entry removed, contact us through the Contact page.
